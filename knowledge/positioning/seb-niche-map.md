# Seb Niche Map

This is the current positioning map based on the `operator-hub` search-demand runs that actually held up after runtime and parser fixes.

## Core position

Build AI, automation, and smarter internal systems for small and medium-sized businesses.

This should be the main frame.
It is broad enough to cover what you can actually deliver, but specific enough to be commercially legible.

## Primary niche

### 1. AI + business efficiency

Best signal so far:

- `ai konsult företag`
- `chatgpt företag`
- `digitalisera processer företag`
- `effektivisera administration företag`
- `ai automation for small business`
- `workflow automation for small business`

What this means:

- lead with practical AI for companies
- focus on time savings, admin reduction, and operational clarity
- avoid abstract “AI innovation” language
- prefer “make work faster, cleaner, and less manual”

Suggested commercial framing:

- AI workflows for real company work
- practical AI for administration, support, internal operations, and repeatable tasks
- help companies use ChatGPT and AI tools in a structured way

### 2. Integrations and connected systems

Best signal so far:

- `systemintegration företag`
- `koppla ihop system företag`
- `crm integration företag`
- `business systems integration`

What this means:

- this is the strongest concrete delivery lane under the main AI/automation frame
- it makes the offer feel real, technical, and implementation-driven
- it connects well to M365, CRM, forms, inboxes, dashboards, and internal workflows

Suggested commercial framing:

- connect the tools your company already uses
- remove double work and manual copy-paste between systems
- build flows between CRM, email, Microsoft 365, dashboards, and internal operations

## Secondary service lanes

### 3. Internal tools and dashboards

This is a strong secondary lane, not the best homepage headline.

Use it as:

- proof of technical depth
- a natural next step after AI/automation/integration work
- a service for companies that need better visibility and control

Suggested framing:

- internal tools that fit the company instead of forcing the company into generic tools
- dashboards for operational clarity
- custom workflows for teams with messy processes

### 4. Websites and product surfaces

This should stay in the offer, but not as the main niche.

Why:

- website/app/frontend/backend language is too broad and too crowded
- it is better positioned as an execution capability inside a stronger business frame

Use it as:

- “we also build the interface layer”
- landing pages, portals, internal tools, client-facing workflow surfaces

## Differentiators

### 5. Smart systems and hardware

This is useful, but best as an edge and proof layer.

Examples:

- Home Assistant
- cameras
- routers
- local systems
- smart environments

What it signals:

- you understand full-stack systems in the real world
- you can think from physical operations to software automation
- you are stronger than a pure web freelancer on systems thinking

Do not lead with this as the main niche yet.
Use it as:

- credibility
- case material
- a differentiator for certain clients

## Positioning hierarchy

### Main headline level

- AI, automation, and smarter systems for companies

### Strong delivery pillars

- AI workflows and practical AI adoption
- system integrations and connected operations
- internal tools and dashboards

### Supporting capabilities

- websites, interfaces, and app surfaces
- M365, email, CRM, API work
- smart systems and hardware-aware automation

## What not to lead with

- generic web development
- generic app development
- frontend/backend as standalone positioning
- hardware / IoT as the first thing people hear

Those can stay in the delivery stack, but they weaken the commercial story if they become the headline.

## Recommended homepage-level message

### Main idea

Help companies save time, reduce manual work, and connect their systems using AI, automation, and custom internal tools.

### Short version

AI, automation, and smarter systems for companies.

### Expanded version

I help companies use AI in practical ways, automate repetitive work, connect their systems, and build internal tools that create clarity and save time.
