# Seb Content Map

This content map follows the niche direction that has the strongest signal right now:

- AI + business efficiency
- integrations and connected systems
- internal tools and dashboards
- smart systems as a differentiator

The goal is not just traffic.
The goal is to make your positioning legible and commercially useful.

## Content pillars

### 1. AI in real company work

Use this pillar to attract companies that want practical AI use, not hype.

Topics:

- how companies actually use ChatGPT in daily work
- where AI saves time in administration
- how to introduce AI in a small business without chaos
- what AI should and should not automate

### 2. Automation and process improvement

Use this pillar to connect AI to operational pain.

Topics:

- reduce repetitive admin
- automate handoffs between people and tools
- remove manual copy-paste and duplicated work
- design cleaner workflows

### 3. System integrations

Use this pillar for concrete, technical commercial intent.

Topics:

- CRM integrations
- M365 and inbox workflows
- connect forms, email, CRM, docs, and dashboards
- when to integrate versus replace a system

### 4. Internal tools and dashboards

Use this pillar to show that you can build custom systems when off-the-shelf tools are not enough.

Topics:

- when an internal tool is worth building
- dashboard design for real operations
- how to avoid spreadsheet chaos
- how to create better visibility across a company

### 5. Smart systems and infrastructure edge

Use this pillar carefully.
It should differentiate you, not replace the core positioning.

Topics:

- connecting physical systems and software
- Home Assistant in company environments
- what “smart systems” really mean in operations
- when sensors, devices, or local systems make sense

## Tone and angle

Prefer:

- practical
- operational
- specific
- business-readable

Avoid:

- vague “future of AI” content
- generic developer blogging
- pure trend commentary without operational value

## CTA direction

Articles should naturally lead toward one of these outcomes:

- identify a workflow worth automating
- identify systems that should be connected
- identify where AI can save time
- identify when a custom internal tool is justified

That keeps the content tied to real service intent instead of just traffic.
